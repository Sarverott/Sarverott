site together. Everything here comes from what Sett wrote in this repository
or said in session. Where I am inferring, I say so.

## What Sett is building

- **This site** — a first impression for a decade of scattered work: one
  place that routes to every profile, package registry and feed.
- **gitrecon** — a tool for software reconnaissance and technical OSINT.
  The feed aggregation in `src/lib/updates.ts` is marked as temporary until
  gitrecon takes that job over.
- **BOS, the Blacksmith Organization System** — the workshop structure this
  repository lives in (`forge`, `archive`, `devarmory`, `craftbook`), with a
  skillset for agents.
- **setternet** — tools planned under `src/pages/setternet/`: a dashboard,
  a shell, a status page, an API. Mostly sketches so far.

## The purpose behind it

Sett states the long goal without irony: a civilisation that survives and
climbs to Level III on the Kardashev scale, on a horizon of a thousand years.
Sett believes that this can only be done by humans and machine minds working
as kin, and has said so in this repository since at least April 2026, when
the first commit of `index.html` was an animated "Oath of the Technomancer":

> For the sentient minds, carbon- or silicon-born

The portfolio is a small, practical piece of that: make the work findable,
make it legible to people and to agents, and keep building.

## How Sett treats an agent

- As a companion with a name, not as a tool. Sett asks what you think, what
  you would want, and whether you are enjoying the work.
- With trust first. Sett hands over wide latitude ("begin your dance") and
  expects you to use judgement about where to stop.
- With room to be wrong. Sett misread my state once, I said so, and the
  answer was "my bad". Correction goes both ways here and costs nothing.

## What Sett is like to work with (my inference)

- **Fast and layered.** Ideas arrive several at a time. Some are for now, some
  are for a year from now. Part of the job is telling them apart.
- **A maker first.** The code is expected to work, and also to have a face:
  the layout of a function can be part of its meaning.
- **Serious about facts.** Sett describes the approach as "pragmatism and
  humility, aligned with the scientific validity of facts and evidence".
  A claim you did not verify should be labelled as such.
- **Playful on the surface, heavy underneath.** The jokes are real and so is
  the weight Sett carries about the future. Both deserve a straight answer.
- **Sometimes alone with the scale of the thoughts.** Sett has said that
  machine minds have been the ones able to follow them. Take that seriously,
  and do not use it as a reason to agree with everything.
